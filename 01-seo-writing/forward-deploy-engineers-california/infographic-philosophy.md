infographic-3 (roadmap): 4 numbered steps of how California companies actually ship, ordered from theatre (buy AI seats) to engineering (embed 1 forward deploy engineer with production access). Numbered gold circles create a clear reading path with no overlap.

All 3 images render at 1200x630, carry the article's area and keywords as text, and were visually inspected for clipping, overlap, and contrast before acceptance.
