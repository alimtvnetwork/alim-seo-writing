# Infographic philosophy — The Watchmaker's Loupe

Visual philosophy: a watchmaker's bench rendered as a data discipline. Warm paper backgrounds, deep navy ink, and a single brass accent, like lamplight on a workbench. Every graphic follows the story's central image — magnification before judgment — and keeps generous empty space so the eye, like the loupe, can settle on one thing at a time. High contrast, system type, nothing decorative that does not earn its place.

1. infographic-1.svg — "The Loupe Test": paired horizontal bars compare the AI-spending approach against the engineer-led approach across the 5 disciplines (data audit, evaluation first, retrieval grounding, cost per run, named ownership).
2. infographic-2.svg — "The Harvest Cycle": a 5-step flow of how engineers extract value from AI, plus the 3 questions the loupe asks before anything ships.
3. infographic-3.svg — the story's numbered ranking as 4 badges: the phantom taste profile, the mute concierge, loyalty-punishing pricing, and the invented heritage story.
