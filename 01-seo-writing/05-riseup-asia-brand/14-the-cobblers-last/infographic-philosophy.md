# Infographic philosophy — The Cobbler's Last

Visual philosophy: a workbench rendered as a manufacturing discipline. Warm paper backgrounds, deep navy ink, brass accents like lamplight on wooden lasts. Every graphic contrasts rendered pixels against buildable shoes, keeping generous empty space so the gap between imagination and factory is unmistakable. High contrast, system type, nothing decorative that does not earn its place.

1. infographic-1.svg — "Pixels Are Not Prototypes": paired horizontal bars compare the generative design platform against the cobbler's craft (buildable designs, dyeable colorways, verified claims, zero-rework designs).
2. infographic-2.svg — "The Footwear Numbers": three stat cards — 500 silhouettes generated, 12 designs no factory could build, six figures spent validating pixels.
3. infographic-3.svg — the story's numbered ranking as 4 badges: the unlastable dozen, the phantom cushioning, the undyeable colorways, the sentiment silhouette.
