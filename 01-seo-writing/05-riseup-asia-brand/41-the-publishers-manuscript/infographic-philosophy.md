# Infographic philosophy — The Publisher's Manuscript

Visual philosophy: a back room of manuscripts — paper backgrounds, ink-purple accents, deep navy type. The graphics keep returning to the story's tension: what the machine was trained on versus what the editor actually knows. Layouts are quiet and bookish, with wide margins of empty space, because the subject is taste and taste needs room. System type, high contrast, short labels.

1. infographic-1.svg — "What the Scorer Learned — and What the Editor Knows": coverage bars for the bestseller-trained signals the model absorbed, set against the four editorial instincts it never encoded (the odd voice, the unclassifiable, margin-note instinct, the maybe pile).
2. infographic-2.svg — "The Maybe Pile, Engineered": a 5-step editor-led triage pipeline built on retrieval over the house's own reader reports, ending where it must — the pencil decides.
3. infographic-3.svg — the story's numbered ranking as 5 badges: training on success, throughput vs taste, lost margin notes, author trust, and cost blindness.
