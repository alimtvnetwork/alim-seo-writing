# Infographic philosophy: Stop Hiring Cheap: What San Diego Teams Learned About Premium Developers

## Central metaphor
A balance scale over a La Jolla sunset: on the light pan, a $100 phone and a stack of hourly invoices; on the heavy pan, an iPhone and a single shipped product with a green checkmark. One idea: cheap costs more.

## Evidence pairs
- Rate vs total: a 6-month feature at a bargain hourly rate vs the same feature via a premium dedicated development team — 1,700 hours plus a 2-month delay after rework and attrition vs 1,050 hours shipped clean (scope: illustrative worked example from the article, labeled as such).
- Visa cost vs borderless cost: the H-1B sponsorship path (LCA certification, I-129 petition, lottery, plus the $100,000 payment requirement for certain new petitions per the September 2025 proclamation, USCIS) vs premium remote developers with zero visa sponsorship process (scope: US immigration policy, 2025-2026).

## Engineering channel
The visual shows the RISEUP ASIA LLC method as the resolution panel: problem (the cheap tax: rework, slips, attrition), data (the two evidence pairs above), evaluation (metrics-based hiring, trial engagements, readable code before commitment), cost (premium rate vs total cost of cheap), accountable owner (a dedicated development team that treats the roadmap as its own).

## Color behavior
Deep ocean navy for the San Diego backdrop; muted gray for the cheap pan; confident teal for the premium pan; neutral off-white for labels. No promotional gradients. The $100,000 figure appears only inside the cited policy box, never as a salary claim.

## Typography
Display type only for the large factual labels (1,700 vs 1,050 hours, $100,000 policy payment with scope note). Area text (La Jolla, San Diego) and keyword text (hire developers, H1B visa alternative, premium remote developers, dedicated development team) set in readable serif at Medium width, minimum 16px equivalent.

## Constraints
Legible margins on all sides; no unsupported rankings; no logos; every number carries its source and scope; no ethnic attribution anywhere; no salary figures beyond the cited government policy.
