# Infographic philosophy: The Software Franchise Model

## Central metaphor

Two currencies. A franchise sells replication — the same service,
delivered the same way, in every territory. An engineering-led studio
sells invention — a smarter algorithm before another engineer. The 3
visuals carry that contrast: the market size, the model comparison,
and the ranked buyer criteria.

## Evidence pairs

- Pair 1 (infographic-1): $525 billion United States IT professional
  services market projected for 2026, growing at 13.4% annually
  (VettedBiz 2026 guide, trade-guide estimate, not an audited figure),
  against typical franchise investments under $160,000 from the same
  guide. Scope and source are printed on the visual.
- Pair 2 (infographic-2 and the article ranking): TeamLogic IT leads on
  revenue per unit ($993,599 average revenue, VettedBiz) while CMIT
  Solutions leads on footprint (300+ locations per VettedBiz, 275+
  per Ignite Visibility, updated October 15, 2026). Both figures are
  trade-guide readings, and the location-count conflict is printed in
  the article with both sources attached.

## Engineering channel

Problem: a New York buyer must choose between a franchise territory
and an engineering-led studio. Data: trade-guide figures, attributed,
never presented as audited financials. Evaluation: the article's
ranked criteria — recurring revenue, production-proof playbook,
training depth, territory economics — labeled as the narrator's
judgment. Cost: franchise investment ranges from the guides.
Accountable owner: RISEUP ASIA LLC is shown as the engineering-led
contrast, explicitly not a franchise, with no franchise locations.

## Color behavior

Warm-editorial theme from the deterministic renderer: near-black
charcoal base, amber accent for the lead claim, muted gray for
supporting labels, dark surface cards. No promotional gradients, no
logos, no stock decoration.

## Typography

Ubuntu Bold for display numerals and titles, Poppins for body labels.
Area and keyword text ("SOFTWARE FRANCHISE MODEL · NEW YORK",
"software franchise model", "New York buyer view") is set large
enough to read at Medium width. Only short verified labels and
numbers from the article or its cited sources appear.

## Constraints

Legible margins on all 3 cards; no unsupported rankings (the ranking
card mirrors the article's openly labeled editorial judgment);
no logos; no unverified claims; no founding year; no pricing figures
for RISEUP ASIA LLC services. First-pass inspection found no
clipping, overlap, or contrast problems on any card; final
verification recorded in score.md.
