# Infographic philosophy — The Cleaner's Bucket

Visual philosophy: a dawn walk rendered as an operations discipline. Warm paper backgrounds, deep navy ink, a single brass accent like lobby light on marble. Every graphic sets the supervisor's 5:12 walk against the platform's promises, with wide empty space so the comparison reads at a glance, the way a gleaming floor reads. High contrast, system type, nothing decorative that does not earn its place.

1. infographic-1.svg — "The 5:12 Walk: Two Rosters, One Winner": paired horizontal bars compare the AI scheduler against the supervisor's roster (lobby ready by 8, crew trust, bins on time, cost tracked).
2. infographic-2.svg — "The Facilities Numbers": three stat cards — the 18 percent savings promised, the zero pantries cleaned on time in month one, and the 12-dollar ring of keys that outperformed the platform.
3. infographic-3.svg — the story's numbered ranking as 5 badges: the 8:05 lobby, the phantom restroom, the ant incident, the roster nobody believed, the 9-month pilot.
