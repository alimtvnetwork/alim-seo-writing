# 01 — Background Inventory

Measured notes on the three source backgrounds this spec builds on.

## 1. pixheads-purple-bg.png (dark / premium)

- **Canvas mood:** deep violet-black gradient, darker at edges, glowing slightly
  toward the centre-right.
- **Signature element:** an oversized, very low-contrast triangle watermark
  sitting behind everything — recognisable at a glance without stealing focus.
- **Texture:** a handful of tiny white/purple star specks, unevenly scattered.
- **Best for:** hero banners, announcement cards, anything that wants a
  "premium product" feel.
- **Avoid:** small body text — the gradient shifts tone across the canvas.

## 2. blitz-dark-grid-bg.png (dark / technical)

- **Canvas mood:** near-black, cool-neutral, with a soft radial glow dead centre.
- **Signature element:** a faint uniform grid — thin hairlines on a regular
  lattice, barely visible, giving structure without noise.
- **Texture:** essentially flat; the glow is the only movement.
- **Best for:** pricing/stats cards, developer-flavoured content, quote cards.
- **Avoid:** anything that needs warmth or playfulness.

## 3. magnet-board reference (light / playful)

- **Canvas mood:** very light cool-grey, almost paper. Subtle vertical
  pinstripes run the full height — visible only on close look.
- **Signature element:** big candy-coloured rounded-square "magnet" tiles
  (red, green, purple, photo tile with blue border). Corners are heavily
  rounded (~25% radius); tiles carry a soft drop shadow.
- **Supporting elements:** small white pill badges with a star icon and
  small caps text; a segmented language pill; a dark rounded toolbar strip.
- **Typography on it:** extra-bold rounded sans for the headline
  ("Magnet Board"), medium grey rounded sans for sub-copy, coloured pill
  labels for languages.
- **Best for:** kid/family-friendly content, tutorials, listicles, anything
  that should feel approachable.
- **Avoid:** dark text smaller than ~14px on the pinstripes — use a solid
  white card behind it instead.

## Common DNA across all three

1. **One hero shape per background** (triangle / grid / pinstripes) — never two.
2. **Edges go darker (dark bgs) or stay flat (light bgs)** — keeps the middle
   as the stage for type.
3. **Texture is subtle** — specks, hairlines, pinstripes all sit under 10%
   contrast so text wins.
