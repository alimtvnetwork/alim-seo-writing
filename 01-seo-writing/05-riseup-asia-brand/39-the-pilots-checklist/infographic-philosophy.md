Visual philosophy for The Pilot's Checklist.

One rule governs all three frames: laminated-card clarity. Deep navy ground, gold accents, plain system sans-serif large enough to read at 3 a.m. in a noisy operations center. Every element load-bearing, nothing decorative.

infographic-1.svg: "Airline Tech Spend, 2025" — two bars ($36B airline IT, $50.8B industry-wide) showing the scale of the bet the industry is placing.

infographic-2.svg: "60-80%" — one huge figure for crew-related delays avoided by disciplined scheduling (Dartmouth), with the $13B savings line underneath, because checklists with math beat hype.

infographic-3.svg: "Checklist Discipline" — a 4-step flow (verify every line, AI advises, unify data first, dispatcher decides) mirroring the captain's pre-flight ritual.
