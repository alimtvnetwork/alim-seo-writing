# Infographic Philosophy — Dedicated Development Teams in Sheridan: What Actually Works

The story argues that a dedicated team is proven by shipped customer value, not green dashboards, using the forklift test. The infographics carry the market scale, the pod-versus-team contrast, and the five ownership tests.

## Infographic 1 — the stat card

The market-scale stat dominates because the story is about a booming buying pattern; the 2033 projection shows the trajectory.

## Infographic 2 — the compare card

Two columns mirror the story contrast: rented pods versus owned teams, the choice facing Sheridan buyers.

## Infographic 3 — the ranking card

The story ranking reproduced 1:1 with its criterion and one-line WHY entries under navy number badges.

## Shared design language

All 3 use the same 1200x630 canvas, navy header band carrying the article title (which contains the target area and focus keywords), system sans-serif type, and the "RISEUP ASIA LLC · story series" footer. High contrast, generous whitespace, short labels, no overlap.
