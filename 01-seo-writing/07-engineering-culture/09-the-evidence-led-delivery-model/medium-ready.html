So here is my personal ranking, one narrator's editorial judgment, of the 5 stages of evidence-led delivery in the order they must happen. The criterion is proof produced before promises made.

  1. Measure the baseline comes 1st, because no baseline means no claim. Alim Ul Karim's San Diego engagements begin with instrumentation, and the systems architect who built custom Golang frameworks and enterprise-grade ORMs treats logging as the first deliverable.
