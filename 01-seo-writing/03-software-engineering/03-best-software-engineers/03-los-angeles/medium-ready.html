The scoring criterion is one Alim Ul Karim suggested to me: which signals actually predict a Los Angeles engineer who ships. Here are 4 signals I trust.

  1. They narrate their worst production incident minute by minute. What broke, what they tried, what finally worked, what they changed afterward. WHY: anyone can describe success, but only engineers who owned failures can describe the system honestly, and Los Angeles needs honest systems.
