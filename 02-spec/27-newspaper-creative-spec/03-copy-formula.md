# 03 — Copy Formula

The persuasive skeleton underneath the sample. Five blocks, always in
this order. Works in any language — the sample is Bengali, the formula
is universal.

## The five blocks

1. **Brand + opportunity.** `[Brand] + [hiring word] [role]!`
   - Sample: "Design Monks এ লীড মার্কেটিং ম্যানেজার নিচ্ছি!"
   - English: "Design Monks is hiring a Lead Marketing Manager!"
   - Keep the brand in its native script; write the rest in the
     audience's language. Code-switching is a feature.

2. **Salary hook.** `[Salary word], [figure] [up-to]!`
   - Sample: "স্যালারি ১ লাখ পর্যন্ত!" — the figure is the biggest
     type on the page. Never bury the number in a sentence.

3. **Bonus / sweetener.** `[Condition], [reward]!`
   - Sample: "সাকসেসফুল রেফার করলে পাবেন ১০ হাজার টাকা বোনাস!"
   - A second money figure doubles the reason to stop scrolling.
     Referral bonus, joining bonus, WFH stipend — whatever is true.

4. **(Optional) proof line.** One short credibility phrase —
   "Trusted by 500+ teams", "A concern of X". Skip if space is tight;
   the sample skips it.

5. **CTA.** `[Details] [where]`
   - Sample: "বিস্তারিত কমেন্টে" (Details in the comments) + ▼.
   - English: "Details in the comments" / "Apply at link in bio".
   - Always a location, never just "Apply now" floating alone.

## Highlighting discipline

- Highlight **at most 4 phrases** per creative: brand, role, salary
  figure, bonus figure. The sample highlights exactly these four.
- Highlight the *figure*, not the whole sentence: "১ লাখ" not
  "স্যালারি ১ লাখ পর্যন্ত".
- The red underline belongs to the single most important number
  (usually salary). One red accent per creative.

## Multilingual notes

- **Numerals:** use the audience's native numerals (১ লাখ, not 1 lakh)
  when the script has them — it reads as "for us".
- **Mixed script:** brand names and loanwords ("স্যালারি", "রেফার")
  stay in their familiar form; don't purify the language.
- **RTL languages (Arabic, Urdu):** mirror the layout — ornaments flip
  sides, but the block order stays top-to-bottom.
- **Length check:** German and Bengali compounds run long. If a
  highlighted phrase wraps to three lines, shorten the copy, not the
  type size.
