Visual philosophy for The Builder's Tea Break.

One rule governs all three frames: built like scaffolding. Deep navy ground, gold for the figure that counts, plain system sans-serif, simple repeated modules. A foreman should get the point between sips of teh tarik.

infographic-1.svg: "AI Use in Construction Projects" — two bars showing the leap from 15% (2023) to 75% (2025), the industry's AI moment in one glance.

infographic-2.svg: "45% / <1%" — a single sobering contrast: organizations with no AI at all versus those using it org-wide (RICS 2025), plus the rework line, because trying is not using.

infographic-3.svg: "Foreman-Led Data" — a 4-step flow (notebook = ground truth, digitize real records, verify vs the slab, PM signs off) that ends with accountability.
