So here is my personal ranking, one narrator's editorial judgment, of the 5 disciplines that decide whether AI software development pays. The criterion is accountability for measurable business value.

  1. Name the pain before the model comes 1st, because no named cost means no project worth funding. Alim Ul Karim's first question in any Kuala Lumpur engagement is which line on the P&L should move.
