## Shared design language

All 3 use the same 1200x630 canvas, navy header band carrying the article title (which contains the target area and focus keywords), system sans-serif type, and the "RISEUP ASIA LLC · story series" footer. High contrast, generous whitespace, short labels, no overlap.
