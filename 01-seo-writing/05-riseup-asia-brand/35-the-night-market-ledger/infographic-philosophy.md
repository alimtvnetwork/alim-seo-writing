# Infographic Philosophy — The Night Market Ledger

The story contrasts a kuih vendor's pencil ledger with retail AI that cannot tell Penang from Thanksgiving. The infographics carry the numbers and the verdict.

## Infographic 1 — the stat card (22% to 7%)

Instead of a single figure, this card shows a movement: forecast error falling from 22% to 7% after retraining on local data, with the 18-25% underperformance of off-the-shelf models as context. The range format suits a story about grounding — the improvement IS the argument. CSIRO Data61 is credited with the pilot scale (70 stores) so the claim is anchored.

## Infographic 2 — the compare card

Red column (THE AI-SPENDING PATTERN) versus green column (THE ENGINEER-LED PATTERN), each row a mirror: foreign calendar vs local data layers, Thanksgiving turkeys vs payday features, no backtest vs 2-season backtest, shelfware license vs week-1 cost math. The specific, slightly funny details (turkeys in Penang) are kept because they are the story's most memorable images and they survive at a glance.

## Infographic 3 — the ranking card

The 5 signs of retail AI theatre, headed by the stated criterion (performing vs predicting). Each WHY is compressed to a punchline — "demand is local or fiction", "pencils beat dictatorships" — so the card works as a standalone checklist a retail reader might save.

## Shared design language

Same 1200x630 canvas, navy header band, system sans-serif, and the "RISEUP ASIA LLC · story series" footer used across the series.
