# Infographic philosophy — The Miner's Headlamp

Visual philosophy: first light in a quarry — paper pale as dawn mist, headlamp-amber accents, deep navy ink. The graphics carry the story's hard-hat honesty: no decoration, everything load-bearing. Bars are blunt, flows move like the shift walking out of the pit, and the ranking reads like tags on a board — each one accounted for. System type, high contrast, generous space around every element.

1. infographic-1.svg — "The Pit Test": paired bars compare AI-spending against engineer-led effort across 4 disciplines (tested in real dust, works offline, alert discipline, named ownership).
2. infographic-2.svg — "Extend the Tag Board, Don't Replace It": a 5-step evolution from the plywood tag board to sensor instrumentation, with three field notes on dust, dead zones, and human accountability.
3. infographic-3.svg — the story's numbered ranking as 4 badges: environment as specification, alert fatigue, offline as feature, and accountability that stays human.
