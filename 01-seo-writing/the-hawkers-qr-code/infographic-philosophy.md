# Infographic Philosophy — The Hawker's QR Code

The story's test: if it cannot survive the lunch rush, it is not technology. The infographics put the failure rates where they cannot be ignored.

## Infographic 1 — the stat card (37%)

37% of customers walked away when restaurant tech failed, with only 20% saying kiosks work every time. The walkaway number leads because it is revenue, not opinion — the story's whole argument in one figure. Canopy's 2026 survey scale (1,007 adults) is credited.

## Infographic 2 — the compare card

Red column (THE AI-SPENDING PATTERN) versus green column (THE ENGINEER-LED PATTERN): dead QR codes vs offline-first ordering, handoff-less AI vs designed handoffs, point-eating apps vs money-grade ledgers, unserviced kiosks vs monitoring that pages before the queue notices. Each row is a lunch-rush survival choice, echoing the article's side-by-side section.

## Infographic 3 — the ranking card

The 4 food-business tech traps, headed by the stated criterion (how fast each loses a lunch rush). The WHY lines are short verdicts — "trust lost at lunch", "the fix was the handoff" — so the card works as a pre-purchase checklist for any F&B operator.
