# Infographic Philosophy

The $2M Software Franchise Coming to Washington DC (franchise-washington-dc)

Visual philosophy: three restrained editorial panels, one idea each, built on a 1200x630 canvas with system sans-serif type (DejaVu Sans), generous whitespace, and high-contrast colour fields. No stock imagery, no decorative clutter. Every label and number appears in the article or its cited sources. The footer "RISEUP ASIA LLC · story series" is kept small and discreet.

- infographic-1 (The $2M model): a left-to-right flow of three panels, $2M in, 50% of the profit margin, 5 years or break-even, on deep navy with a gold rule. It communicates the franchise offer as a simple machine: money in, shared margin, a clock.
- infographic-2 (The GenAI divide): two opposing panels, 95% no measurable return in muted red against 5% reaching production in muted green, with the MIT Project NANDA 2025 attribution. It communicates why Washington DC needs engineers, not licences.
- infographic-3 (Franchising by the numbers): three white stat cards on warm paper, $921.4B output, 845,000 units, 8.9M jobs from the IFA 2026 outlook, plus the Washington DC-specific franchise fact. It communicates that franchising is a measured, mainstream economy, not a gamble.

QA: each SVG parsed as XML; each PNG rendered at 1200x630 and inspected for clipped or overlapping text, weak contrast, and missing glyphs. Layouts use centred type with wide margins so no label touches an edge or another element.
