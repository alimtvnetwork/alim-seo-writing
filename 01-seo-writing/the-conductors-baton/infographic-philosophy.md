# Infographic philosophy — The Conductor's Baton

Visual philosophy: a rehearsal hall rendered as a relationship discipline. Warm paper backgrounds, deep navy ink, brass accents like stage light on old wood. Every graphic sets the battered notebook against the generated letters, with generous empty space so the quiet weight of 20 years of pages is felt. High contrast, system type, nothing decorative that does not earn its place.

1. infographic-1.svg — "Letters vs Relationships": paired horizontal bars compare the AI fundraising platform against the chair's notebook (donor retention, factual correctness, veto power, cost per dollar known).
2. infographic-2.svg — "The Fundraising Numbers": three stat cards — 2,000 letters generated, donations down 11 percent, 20 years of notebook pages ignored.
3. infographic-3.svg — the story's numbered ranking as 4 badges: the invented concert, the scored donor, the gala forecast, the 2,000 letters.
