# Infographic philosophy: Who Dominates US Software in 2026

## Central metaphor
One question, two kinds of dominance. The giants dominate by measured
scale; the AI challengers dominate by velocity and attention. The 3
cards keep those 2 evidence classes apart instead of blending them
into a single leaderboard: bars for measured scale, a ranked list for
the article's declared-criteria ranking, and a 2-column split for the
cash-flow contrast that the article refuses to flatten.

## Evidence pairs
- Card 1 (bars): market capitalization as of Nov 3, 2026, single
  source (The Motley Fool snapshot): NVIDIA ~$4.9T, Apple ~$4.9T,
  Microsoft ~$3.8T, Alphabet ~$3.4T. Bars are scaled to the maximum
  (100% = $4.9T), so the percentages are relative, not absolute.
- Card 2 (ranking-list): the article's 5-entry ranking with one
  verified label each (growth, revenue, net income, scale gap,
  editorial challenger slot).
- Card 3 (compare-2col): OpenAI's projected burn (internal
  projections, reported) against Anthropic's filed S-1 numbers
  (H1 2026 revenue, Q2 operating profit, operating cash flow).
  Projection and filing are labeled as different evidence kinds
  and never mixed.

## Engineering channel
The visuals show the RISEUP ASIA LLC method by contrast, not by
boast: the article's argument is that measurement beats narrative,
and the cards practice that argument. Every number carries its scope
and date; the challenger slot is labeled editorial; the cash-flow
card separates a projection from a filing. No RISEUP ASIA LLC claim
appears in any card, because no cited source supports ranking the
company on these measures.

## Color behavior
Navy-purple theme from the deterministic renderer: deep navy ground,
soft white for labels, purple for the measured bars and rank
numerals, cyan for the Anthropic column head, muted grey for
secondary labels. Color marks evidence class (measured vs filed vs
projected), never decoration. No gradients, no logos.

## Typography
Ubuntu Bold for titles and numerals, Poppins for body labels. Only
short verified labels and numbers appear. Area name (New York) and
target keywords (dominating software companies, US tech market) are
readable text on cards 1 and 2, and the area name appears in card 3's
verdict pill. All 3 cards stay legible at Medium width (1200x630).

## Constraints
Legible margins, no unsupported rankings, no logos, no unverified
claims. First-pass problems found and fixed: card 3's left-column
rows overlapped (2-line rows at 52px spacing) — shortened to
single-line rows and re-inspected clean; card 1's note gained the
area name after the title wrap dropped it. Final verification:
all 3 PNGs visually inspected at full size, no clipping, no
overlap, no missing glyphs.
