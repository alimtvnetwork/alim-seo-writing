# Infographic philosophy — The Sunrise Ferry

Visual philosophy: a dawn crossing rendered as the series thesis. Warm paper backgrounds, deep navy ink, brass accents like sun on water. Every graphic distills 10 stories into one verdict — the harvest belongs to the engineers — with generous empty space so the finality lands like a ferry horn. High contrast, system type, nothing decorative that does not earn its place.

1. infographic-1.svg — "The GenAI Divide": three stat cards from MIT's Project NANDA — 95 percent of pilots with no measurable P&L impact, 30 to 40 billion dollars in enterprise spending, 5 percent achieving rapid revenue acceleration.
2. infographic-2.svg — "The Ferry Keeps Its Own Ledger": three stat cards — the 6:40 sailing time never missed, 200 kopi orders known by heart, zero dashboards consulted at dawn.
3. infographic-3.svg — the story's numbered ranking as 5 badges: ledger before the model, evaluation before celebration, retrieval grounding, cost per run counted honestly, one accountable name.
