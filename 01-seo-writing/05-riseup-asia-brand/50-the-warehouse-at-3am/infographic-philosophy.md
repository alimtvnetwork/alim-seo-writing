# Infographic Philosophy — The Warehouse at 3 AM

The story's argument: logistics AI fails on dirty data, and the fix is unglamorous data engineering. The infographics make the data-quality gap impossible to ignore.

## Infographic 1 — the stat card (23%)

Only 23% of logistics firms say 75-100% of their data is clean, with nearly 1 in 3 admitting less than half is trustworthy. The smallness of the number is the point — it reframes every routing-AI pitch as a bet on data the buyer does not have. The Ontegos Cloud survey scale (800+ professionals) is credited for weight.

## Infographic 2 — the compare card

Red column (THE AI-SPENDING PATTERN) versus green column (THE ENGINEER-LED PATTERN): optimize-first vs audit-first, renamed streets vs driver-validated geocodes, fantasy warehouse vs digitized local knowledge, stale feeds vs measured freshness. Each row pairs one of Rahim's real problems with the engineering answer, keeping the story's characters present even in the graphic.

## Infographic 3 — the ranking card

The 4 logistics AI failure modes with the stated criterion (how silently each burns money). The WHY lines are kept terse — "the gospel arrives late", "fantasy warehouse" — because the card's job is to be memorable, not exhaustive; the article carries the detail.
