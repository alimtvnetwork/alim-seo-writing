# Infographic Philosophy — The Pharmacist's Question

The story's moral: in healthcare, the question asked before harm beats the answer given after. The infographics translate clinical caution into numbers and rules.

## Infographic 1 — the stat card (23%)

Only 23% of 4,600 clinical AI papers used real-world patient data, with just 19 prospective randomized trials. The number indicts the evidence base, not any single product — which matches the story's argument that certification is running ahead of proof. Nature Medicine is credited plainly.

## Infographic 2 — the compare card

Red column (THE AI-SPENDING PATTERN) versus green column (THE ENGINEER-LED PATTERN): billing-code triage vs protocol-grounded retrieval, bolted-on sign-off vs designed-in sign-off, untested certification vs hardest-case evaluation, unpriced liability vs audit-first architecture. The rows mirror the story's side-by-side section and its 5 rules in compressed form.

## Infographic 3 — the ranking card

The 5 rules for AI in healthcare, headed by the stated criterion (patient-safety leverage per rule). The WHY lines are aphoristic — "liability needs a license", "refusal is a feature" — because in healthcare the memorable rule is the one that gets followed at 3 AM.
