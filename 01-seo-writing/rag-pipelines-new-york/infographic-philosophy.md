# Infographic Philosophy — RAG Pipelines in New York: What Actually Works

Visual philosophy: calm, evidence-first panels on a warm paper background. Two
ink colors only (a muted red for the "buying AI tools" path and a deep teal for
the "engineer-led delivery" path) so the contrast between theatre and
engineering reads at a glance. Big numerals carry the verified research figures
(MIT NANDA, Gartner, McKinsey) because numbers anchor the argument better than
adjectives. Short labels, generous whitespace, no decorative clutter, no stock
art. The company name sits discreetly in the footer. Every chart contains the
target area (New York) and the focus topic in readable text so the
images work as standalone SEO assets.

- infographic-2: the three research figures that frame RAG pipelines decisions in New York, as honest horizontal bars.
