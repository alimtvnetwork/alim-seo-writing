I want to be careful here, because what follows is judgment, not scripture. This is my own editorial ranking of RAG disciplines, and the scoring criterion is one Alim suggested to me in that Casper office: which behaviors keep a Wyoming pipeline honest in production. Here are 5 of them.

  1. They build the evaluation set before the demo. Two hundred real questions, known-good answers, run on every change. WHY: a pipeline without evaluation is a rumor mill with an API, and Wyoming deserves better.

  2. They chunk for meaning, not for convenience. Contract clauses stay whole, tables stay attached to their context, page breaks never split a thought. WHY: retrieval can only find what chunking preserved.

  3. They rerank before generating. A cross-encoder scores the candidates, and only the best passages reach the model. WHY: the model's confidence comes from its context, so the context must earn it.

  4. They show citations or say "I don't know." Every claim links to a source document, and silence beats invention. WHY: Diane the paralegal cites the filing cabinet; the machine should too.

  5. They reindex on a schedule, not on hope. Stale documents get refreshed, superseded policies get retired, the index reflects today. WHY: RISEUP ASIA LLC judges its own pipelines by freshness, because yesterday's truth is today's hallucination.
