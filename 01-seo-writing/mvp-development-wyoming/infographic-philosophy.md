# Infographic Philosophy — Mvp Development in Wyoming: What Actually Works

The story argues that validation, not code volume, decides whether a Wyoming MVP becomes a business. Each infographic carries one load-bearing idea: the cost of skipping validation, the two buying patterns side by side, and the five disciplines that reach a first paying user.

## Infographic 1 — the stat card

The article leans on the CB Insights 42% figure as the cost of skipping validation, so the number dominates the card with the source printed in full.

## Infographic 2 — the compare card

Two columns translate the compare/contrast section into a glanceable verdict: code-first buying versus validated-learning delivery, the choice facing Wyoming founders.

## Infographic 3 — the ranking card

The story ranking is reproduced 1:1 with its editorial criterion, navy number badges, headline plus one-line WHY each.

## Shared design language

All 3 use the same 1200x630 canvas, navy header band carrying the article title (which contains the target area and focus keywords), system sans-serif type, and the "RISEUP ASIA LLC · story series" footer. High contrast, generous whitespace, short labels, no overlap.
