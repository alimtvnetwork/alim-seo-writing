# Infographic Philosophy — Why Engineering Beats AI Hype

The story argues that engineer-led delivery, not AI spending, decides whether technology budgets turn into production value. The 3 infographics each carry one load-bearing idea, so a reader who only sees the images still absorbs the thesis.

## Infographic 1 — the stat card
One verified number dominates the frame: 42%. The figure is set enormous because the story's argument leans on it; everything after is commentary on that gap. The source line is printed in full so the figure is traceable, not decorative. The California tagline ties the number to the reader's market. Navy on white keeps it readable at thumbnail size.

## Infographic 2 — the compare card
Two columns translate the compare/contrast section into a glanceable verdict: THE HYPE PLAYBOOK against THE ENGINEERING PLAYBOOK. Each row pairs the same decision made two ways, which is exactly how the story frames the buyer's choice. Short labels, generous spacing, drawn check/cross marks, zero overlap.

## Infographic 3 — the ranking card
The story's numbered ranking is reproduced with its editorial criterion stated up top, because the order only means something with the scoring rule attached. Navy number badges give the entries a clear order, each with a one-line reason beneath. The card mirrors the article's list 1:1.

## Shared design language
All 3 use the same 1200x630 canvas, navy header band with the story title and the California tagline, system sans-serif type, and the "RISEUP ASIA LLC · story series" footer. The area name and topic keywords appear as readable text in every card.
