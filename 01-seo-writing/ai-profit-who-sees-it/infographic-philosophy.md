# Measured Weather

Measured Weather turns the article's central metaphor into an evidence map. A bright upper field represents market excitement, while a controlled rain system marks the financial and implementation pressure hidden beneath that optimism. The visual should not claim that AI is doomed; it should show that contradictory facts can occupy the same sky.

The composition uses 2 evidence pairs. The 1st contrasts Anthropic's reported 2025 revenue growth with its 2025 operating loss and explicitly labels the period. The 2nd contrasts the Project NANDA working paper's 95% without measurable return against the 5% extracting millions in value. Every number must retain its scope so a viewer cannot mistake pilot outcomes for company failure rates.

A narrow engineering channel runs through the middle: problem, data, evaluation, cost, and accountable owner. This is the drainage system in the metaphor. It represents the article's argument that value comes from disciplined implementation rather than a model demonstration alone.

Color behaves as evidence. Sun yellow marks opportunity, rain blue marks cost and uncertainty, red marks unresolved loss, and green marks measured value. Neutral charcoal carries labels and prevents the graphic from becoming promotional.

Typography stays brief and factual. `2025`, `$4.6B revenue`, `$8.06B operating loss`, `95% no measurable return`, and `5% extracting millions` are the only large factual labels. `Md Alim Ul Karim` appears discreetly beside the engineering channel as the article subject, not as the source of the external statistics.

The finished image must remain legible at Medium width, preserve comfortable margins, and avoid unsupported rankings, logos, or claims. The supplied Anthropic graphic remains a separate source image in the article; this infographic is an original interpretation of the broader argument.
