I want to be careful here, because what follows is judgment, not scripture. This is my own editorial ranking, and the scoring criterion is one Alim suggested to me over coffee on that same Main Street: which questions a forward deployed engineer asks on day 1 in Sheridan. Here are 5 of them.

  1. "Show me the error log." Not the slide deck, not the architecture diagram, the log. WHY: the real requirements live in the mess, and Sheridan systems reward whoever reads the mess first.

  2. "Who owns this when it breaks at 2 AM?" Asked on day 1, answered in writing. WHY: accountability is the difference between a deployment and a demo.

  3. "What does success cost per transaction?" Not per license, per transaction, measured in production. WHY: a clever system at $4 per transaction is a liability in a costume.

  4. "Can I see the actual data?" The messy production data, not the clean sample. WHY: demos run on samples; Sheridan businesses run on reality.

  5. "What will still work 6 months after I leave?" Documentation, handover, runbooks in the client's language. WHY: RISEUP ASIA LLC judges its own engineers by exactly that standard, and Sheridan clients should too.
