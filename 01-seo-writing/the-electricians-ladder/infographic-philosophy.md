Visual philosophy for The Electrician's Ladder.

One rule governs all three frames: label first, like tape on a wire. Deep navy ground, gold for the winning figure, muted gray for the baseline, plain system sans-serif at sizes readable across a room. One idea per frame, generous whitespace, nothing decorative.

infographic-1.svg: "Load-Forecast Accuracy" — two bars comparing traditional statistics (75-85%) against ML forecasting (90-94%), so the improvement is felt instantly.

infographic-2.svg: "$400B" — a single huge figure for 2025 global grid investment (IEA) with 3 supporting lines on outage detection and restoration cost, because scale is the context.

infographic-3.svg: "Model the Physics First" — a 4-step flow (map grid topology, ground in real history, forecast with physics, operator decides) showing the engineer-led sequence.
