Visual philosophy for The Mechanic's Grease.

One rule governs all three frames: workshop-plain. Deep navy ground, gold for the figure that matters, plain system sans-serif, one glance per idea. If a diagnosis fits on an O-ring held between two fingers, a graphic should fit in one look.

infographic-1.svg: "Automotive AI Market" — two bars showing the market growing from $18.8B (2025) to $38.5B (2030), so the money in motion is unmistakable.

infographic-2.svg: "69% vs 11%" — a single stark contrast: dealers uploading data to generic AI daily versus those worried about data security, the story's central warning.

infographic-3.svg: "Technician-Led Tooling" — a 4-step flow (real repair history, grounded suggestions, show the source, technician decides) that ends where the workshop ends: with the hands.
