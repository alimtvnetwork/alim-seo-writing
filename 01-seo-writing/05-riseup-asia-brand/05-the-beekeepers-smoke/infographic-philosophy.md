# Infographic philosophy — The Beekeeper's Smoke

Visual philosophy: an apiary rendered as a forecasting discipline. Warm paper backgrounds, deep navy ink, a single brass accent like morning sun on hive boxes. Every graphic sets the water-stained ledger against the smiling dashboard, with generous empty space so the humility of "most years" reads louder than false precision. High contrast, system type, nothing decorative that does not earn its place.

1. infographic-1.svg — "The Valley vs The Dashboard": paired horizontal bars compare the satellite platform against the beekeeper's ledger (forecast accuracy, usable alerts, uncertainty shown, affordable cost).
2. infographic-2.svg — "The Agri Numbers That Worked": three stat cards from the engineer-led Saagu Baagu programme — 21 percent higher chili yields, doubled farmer incomes, 500,000 farmers scaling up.
3. infographic-3.svg — the story's numbered ranking as 5 badges: the 2-decimal harvest, the valley never met, the dashboard with no signal, the 10 percent adoption trap, the ledger never digitized.
