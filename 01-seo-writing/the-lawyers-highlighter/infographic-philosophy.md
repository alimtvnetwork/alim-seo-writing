Visual philosophy for The Lawyer's Highlighter.

One rule governs all three frames: the eye should land on the verdict before the brain starts working. Deep navy ground, gold for the engineer-led answer, muted gray for the status quo, plain system sans-serif at sizes readable across a room. No decoration, no fine print. Like a highlighter, each graphic marks exactly one idea.

infographic-1.svg: "AI Cuts First-Pass Review Time" — two horizontal bars comparing manual review against AI-assisted review (55-65% faster), so the productivity claim is visible in one glance.

infographic-2.svg: "Adoption Up, Impact Flat" — two bars setting 78% AI adoption against under 15% of firms seeing business impact, the gap that is the story's whole argument.

infographic-3.svg: "The Engineer-Led Review" — a 4-step flow (retrieve from firm playbooks, evaluate vs lawyer markups, track cost per run, lawyer signs off) showing the sequence that keeps the human accountable.
