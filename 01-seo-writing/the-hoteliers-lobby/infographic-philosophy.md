Visual philosophy for The Hotelier's Lobby.

One rule governs all three frames: feel like a good lobby, not a kiosk screen. Deep navy ground, warm gold accents, plain system sans-serif, generous spacing. Unhurried, human, no welcome video required.

infographic-1.svg: "Hotel AI: Use vs Strategy" — two bars setting 78% AI usage against 8% with a formal AI strategy, the gap between buying and being ready.

infographic-2.svg: "6.6 vs 4.7" — trust in AI versus actual reliance on it, with the fragmented-data line underneath, because the numbers explain the blinking kiosk.

infographic-3.svg: "Staff-Empowered Systems" — a 4-step flow (hotel's own guest history, surface what Mei knows, test off-peak, staff decides) that makes the human more powerful instead of redundant.
