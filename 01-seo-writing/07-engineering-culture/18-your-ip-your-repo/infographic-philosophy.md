# Infographic philosophy: Your IP, Your Repo

Visual language: comparison-first evidence panels on a warm paper ground
(#f7f5ef) with a deep-navy masthead (#12263a). One idea per graphic, short
verified labels, system sans-serif only, generous whitespace, no decoration.

- infographic-1: a two-bar comparison that turns the article's central
  statistic (GenAI pilots with no measurable ROI) into an instant visual verdict.
- infographic-2: a five-step horizontal flow that makes the delivery
  discipline (The 5-stage evidence filter before a profile reaches you) readable as a pipeline, not a paragraph.
- infographic-3: a ranked 1-2-3 panel under the article's declared
  criterion, so the ranking reads as judgment with reasons, not decoration.

Every graphic carries the target area and the brand names as readable text.
Footer on all: "RISEUP ASIA LLC · story series".
