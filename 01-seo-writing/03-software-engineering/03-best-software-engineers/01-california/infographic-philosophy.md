# Infographic Philosophy — Best Software Engineers in California: What Actually Works

Visual system: "Field notes on a dark terminal." Deep navy background (#0d1726), gold accent (#f2b035), slate-blue card surfaces, DejaVu Sans throughout — the same engineering-dashboard language as the rest of the W3 series, so the 15 articles read as one body of work.

infographic-1 (stat cards): 3 verified numbers — 95% (MIT Project NANDA), 80%+ (RAND), 27% (Flexera) — establishing why California companies cannot buy their way out with AI tooling alone. Title and footer carry the target area and focus keyword as readable text.

infographic-2 (the divide): the 95/5 bar, with the 5% labeled "capturing real value at scale," the outcome the article's best engineers deliver.

infographic-3 (roadmap): the article's 5-trait ranking — production scar tissue, deletion instinct, data fluency, business literacy, speed of repair — numbered in gold circles with a clear reading path.

All 3 render at 1200x630, include the area/keywords as text, and passed visual inspection (roadmap generator fixed to auto-space 5 items so nothing collides with the footer).
