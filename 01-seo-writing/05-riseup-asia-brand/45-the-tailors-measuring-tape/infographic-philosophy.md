# Infographic Philosophy — The Tailor's Measuring Tape

The story's rule: the diagram is a rumor, the body is the fact. The infographics make fit-data discipline the hero.

## Infographic 1 — the stat card (70%)

70% of clothing returns cite size and fit, with the 23.4% US online apparel return rate as context. The number reframes returns as a measurement failure at purchase, not a logistics problem — the story's central inversion. Coresight/Alvanon 2026 is credited.

## Infographic 2 — the compare card

Red column (THE AI-SPENDING PATTERN) versus green column (THE ENGINEER-LED PATTERN): rumor-based recommendations vs measured garments, chart worship vs mapped variance, try-on theatre vs holdout backtesting, logistics framing vs never-shipped returns. The rows mirror the article's side-by-side and its 5 myths in compressed form.

## Infographic 3 — the ranking card

The 5 fashion AI myths, headed by the stated criterion (how much money each myth costs). The WHY lines are tailor-shop aphorisms — "selling the rumor", "tape does not lie" — so the card reads as a buying guide, which is its function in the article.
