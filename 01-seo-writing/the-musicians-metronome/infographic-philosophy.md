# Infographic Philosophy — The Musician's Metronome

Visual approach: supply-versus-demand asymmetry, the dials-versus-curator contrast, and the myths ranked by noise. Navy header, generous whitespace, short labels, footer throughout.

- infographic-1 (the 44% stat): Deezer's AI-upload share set huge against the 1-3% stream share — the supply/demand split in one glance.
- infographic-2 (two paths): dials, demos, and catalog bloat on the red side; labeled skips, curator ground truth, and measured replays on the green side.
- infographic-3 (the ranking): the 5 music AI myths numbered by how completely each confuses making noise with making music.
