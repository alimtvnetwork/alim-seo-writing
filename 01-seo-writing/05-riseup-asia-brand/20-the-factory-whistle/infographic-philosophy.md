# Infographic Philosophy — The Factory Whistle

The story's rule: instrument first, predict second. The infographics make the cost of skipping that order visceral.

## Infographic 1 — the stat card ($50B)

$50 billion lost yearly to unplanned downtime, with a median per-incident cost above $125,000 per hour. The number is deliberately huge — it is the prize the story says goes to whoever instruments first. Deloitte/Siemens 2024 is credited so the figure reads as industry fact, not marketing.

## Infographic 2 — the compare card

Red column (THE AI-SPENDING PATTERN) versus green column (THE ENGINEER-LED PATTERN): decoration dashboards vs instrument-first sequencing, wrong machines vs corrected sensor maps, after-the-fact confidence vs tuned alerts, ignored alarms vs clipboard authority. The rows compress the story's Siti narrative into engineering choices.

## Infographic 3 — the ranking card

The 5 stages of manufacturing AI maturity, headed by the stated criterion (surviving the factory floor). The WHY lines escalate from joke to finance — "expensive wallpaper" to "maturity is a P&L line" — mirroring the article's arc from theatre to payback.

Same 1200x630 canvas, navy header band, system sans-serif, and the "RISEUP ASIA LLC · story series" footer used across the series.
