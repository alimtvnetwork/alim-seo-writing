# 03 — Font Ideas

Font direction drawn from the magnet-board reference (extra-bold rounded
sans headline, friendly rounded sub-copy, pill labels) and tuned to sit
well on the two dark backgrounds as well.

## The shortlist (all free, Google Fonts)

| Role | Pick | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| Display / headlines | **Fredoka** (600–700) | Closest match to the magnet-board headline: geometric, rounded, confident. Carries dark purple and light paper equally. |
| Alt display (warmer) | **Baloo 2** (700–800) | Rounder and bouncier than Fredoka — best for kid/family topics on light backgrounds. |
| Sub-heads / UI labels | **Nunito** (700–800) | Rounded humanist; matches the magnet-board sub-copy and pill labels. |
| Body copy | **Nunito Sans** (400–600) | Pairs with Nunito without the "everything is round" fatigue. |
| Body alt (neutral) | **Inter** (400–600) | When an article needs a serious, invisible body face. |
| Mono accents | **JetBrains Mono** or **IBM Plex Mono** (500) | For stats, URLs, code-ish labels — echoes the `BLITZ-API.AI/PRICING` micro-label on bg 2. |

## Pairings

- **Dark premium (BG-D1, BG-D4):** Fredoka 700 headlines in white, Nunito 600
  sub-copy in `#c9b8e8`, JetBrains Mono for eyebrow labels.
- **Dark technical (BG-D2, BG-D3):** Fredoka 600 or Inter 700 headlines in
  white, Inter 400 body in `#a8a8b3`, mono for numbers.
- **Light playful (BG-L1…L4):** Baloo 2 or Fredoka 700 headlines in `#1a1a2e`,
  Nunito 600 sub-copy in `#4a4a5a`, pill labels in Nunito 800 uppercase
  with letter-spacing.
- **Hybrid (BG-H1…H4):** Fredoka 700 headlines, Nunito body — the safe
  middle that works on both tones.

## Sizing rules

- Display headlines: 700 weight minimum. Rounded fonts look weak at 400–500.
- Eyebrow labels (like "ENDS SUNDAY, OCTOBER 4"): mono or Nunito 800,
  uppercase, letter-spacing 0.15em, 60–70% of body size.
- Never set body text below 16px on textured backgrounds; put a solid card
  behind it instead.
- Pill badges: Nunito 800, sentence case, padding 8px 16px, radius 999px —
  the magnet-board badge pattern.

## What not to do

- No thin/light weights (300) on any background with texture.
- No more than two families per asset (display + body, or display + mono).
- No system serif headlines — the brand voice here is rounded and modern.
