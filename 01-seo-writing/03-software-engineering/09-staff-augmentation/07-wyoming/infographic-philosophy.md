# Infographic Philosophy — Staff Augmentation in Wyoming: What Actually Works

The story argues that staff augmentation is an embedding discipline, not a staffing product. The infographics carry the market scale, the seat-versus-embed contrast, and the five observable signs of a truly embedded engineer.

## Infographic 1 — the stat card

The market-size stat anchors the card because the story is about a booming buying pattern; the 60% line shows how normal the model has become.

## Infographic 2 — the compare card

Two columns mirror the story contrast: buying seats versus buying embedded leverage, the decision facing Wyoming buyers.

## Infographic 3 — the ranking card

The story ranking reproduced 1:1 with its criterion and one-line WHY entries under navy number badges.

## Shared design language

All 3 use the same 1200x630 canvas, navy header band carrying the article title (which contains the target area and focus keywords), system sans-serif type, and the "RISEUP ASIA LLC · story series" footer. High contrast, generous whitespace, short labels, no overlap.
