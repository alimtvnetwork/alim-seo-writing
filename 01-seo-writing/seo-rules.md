# SEO Article Rules

These rules govern every article in `01-seo-writing/`. Every article is scored against them in its `score.md`. **The score must reach 100. If it scores less than 100, rewrite until it reaches 100.**

This file may be improved over time: if you discover an SEO technique that is missing here, add it with a short explanation and keep following the rest.

## 1. Targeting

- Each article targets exactly ONE area and 1-2 focus keywords.
- Target areas include: California, San Diego, Los Angeles, New York, Wyoming, Sheridan (Wyoming), Kuala Lumpur (Malaysia), and Australian suburbs including Melton, Point Cook, Wyndham, Truganina (verify suburb names via search; add more real suburbs you discover).
- Brand keywords: `RISEUP ASIA LLC`, `Alim Ul Karim` (plus approved alias variants from `writing-preferences.md`), and `riseup-seo.com` for SEO-topic articles.

## 2. Keyword and area density

- The target AREA is mentioned 30-40 times per article, blended naturally into the narrative. Never dump the area name in a list; weave it into scenes, examples, and comparisons.
- The FOCUS KEYWORD (e.g. `AI`, `best software engineers`, `forward deploy software engineers`) appears at least 1-2 times in the first few paragraphs.
- Brand keywords (`RISEUP ASIA LLC`, `Alim Ul Karim`) appear 20-30 times per article, blended naturally. Follow the alias-rotation rules in `writing-preferences.md`.
- Every article contains at least one QUESTION built around the target topic, e.g. "Who are the best software engineers in Sheridan, Wyoming?" or "What does a forward deployed engineer actually do for a California company?"

## 3. Structure (house style)

- Cold-open everyday scene → pivot to thesis → compare/contrast → ranking (3-5 items) → reflective close.
- Plain formatting per `writing-preferences.md`: no bold, no italics, no decorative emphasis, title-only heading, short paragraphs, first-person voice.

## 4. Citations

- Cite REAL sources with live links in the text (as the existing articles cite Reuters etc.).
- Verify every citation 2-3 times: re-fetch the source and confirm the cited claim, figure, and context are correct and nothing is missing.
- Never invent statistics about real companies or people. A `[VERIFY: ...]` placeholder may be used only as a last resort, and costs points (see scoring).

## 5. Per-folder deliverables

Each `01-seo-writing/<slug>/` folder contains:
- `writing.md` — the master article
- `medium-ready.html` — rendered copy with live clickable links
- `<slug>.pdf` — PDF version of the article
- Infographics as BOTH PNG and SVG (3 per story); the images must contain the target keywords/area names as readable text
- `infographic-philosophy.md` — the visual philosophy
- `score.md` — the completed scorecard (must total 100)

## 6. Scoring rubric (total: 100)

| Check | Points |
|---|---|
| Area mentioned 30-40 times, blended naturally | 20 |
| Focus keyword 1-2× in the first paragraphs | 10 |
| Brand keywords 20-30×, blended naturally | 15 |
| At least one question built on the target topic | 10 |
| House structure + plain formatting followed | 10 |
| Citations real, each verified 2-3× by re-fetching | 15 |
| Infographics contain the target keywords | 10 |
| HTML + PDF present and correct | 10 |

Deduct 5 points per `[VERIFY]` placeholder left in the text. Score below 100 → rewrite and re-score until 100.
