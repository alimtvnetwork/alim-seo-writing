# Infographic philosophy: The Engineering Edge of RISEUP ASIA LLC

Visual language: comparison-first evidence panels on a dark warm ground
(#0B0A09) with amber accent (#FFAD01) for the claim that matters and
neutral off-white (#F7F5F2) for labels. One idea per graphic, short
verified labels only, system sans-serif only (Ubuntu bold display,
Poppins body), generous whitespace, no decoration, no logos.

- infographic-1: the aim, stated as an aim. Hero "10X–30X" is the
  company's engineering philosophy (an aim, never a measured outcome),
  supported by 2 attributed figures: 180+ pre-vetted engineers (Clutch
  profile) and 50–70 years combined experience (company-described).
  Footer names the area and restates the aim so the hero can never be
  read as a delivered result.
- infographic-2: a 2-column comparison without ranking, under the
  article's declared criteria (algorithm leverage, senior judgment,
  accountable ownership). Left column states the headcount model;
  right column states the algorithm-first model; the verdict pill
  carries the area name.
- infographic-3: a single stat panel for the seniority behind the
  method: 15+ years of experience for Alim Ul Karim (Intch company
  page), under a "CALIFORNIA FOCUS" label.

Every graphic carries the target area and the brand names as readable
text. Target keywords appear as readable text: "RISEUP ASIA LLC
ENGINEERING" (infographic-1 eyebrow), "Software engineering results
for a California client" (infographic-2 subtitle), "CALIFORNIA
SOFTWARE ENGINEERING" (infographic-3 label), and "California" (all
3 graphics).

## Central metaphor
One contrast carries the argument: a bigger team versus a better
algorithm. Every graphic returns to it.

## Evidence pairs
- 180+ pre-vetted engineers (Clutch profile, attributed) against
  50–70 years combined experience (company-described, attributed).
- 15+ years for Alim Ul Karim (Intch company page, attributed) against
  the giants' scale context in the article (Motley Fool table, figures
  as of Nov 3, 2026). No pair mixes sources or periods.

## Engineering channel
Problem first, then logic, then headcount: infographic-2 shows the
method as a choice between 2 models, and infographic-1 shows the aim
that choice serves. No step claims a measured client outcome.

## Color behavior
Amber (#FFAD01) marks the claim that matters (eyebrow, right-column
head, verdict stroke). Off-white (#F7F5F2) carries labels and numerals.
Muted gray (#ADA8A2) carries supporting text. No promotional gradients;
the glow is a faint amber radial, not a color wash.

## Typography
Only large factual labels get display type (Ubuntu bold heroes and
titles). Area and keyword text stays readable at Medium width: smallest
label is 20 px tracked caps, smallest body is 22 px footer text.

## Constraints
Legible margins (renderer self-check: 55 px perimeter, 80 px text
margins, no overlaps), no unsupported rankings, no logos, no unverified
claims, no founding year, no pricing figures, no salary figures.
