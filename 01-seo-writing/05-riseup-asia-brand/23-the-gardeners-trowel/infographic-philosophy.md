# Infographic Philosophy — The Gardener's Trowel

Visual approach: the 5% who extract value, the render-versus-soil contrast, and the gaps ranked by distance from dirt. Navy header, high contrast, one idea per card, footer on each.

- infographic-1 (the 5% stat): MIT's value-extraction figure set huge — the small slice the story argues is engineer-led.
- infographic-2 (two paths): gorgeous renders and invisible drainage on the red side; digitized soil records and logged survival on the green side.
- infographic-3 (the ranking): the 4 green-tech AI gaps numbered by how far each grows from actual soil.
