# Infographic Philosophy — The Insurance Agent's Umbrella

The story's verdict: a promise is only as good as the claims department. The infographics make the denial machine visible.

## Infographic 1 — the stat card (90%)

90% of AI-denied claims reversed on appeal, with 300,000 denied in 2 months at 1.2 seconds each as context. The reversal rate leads because it is the system's own report card — the number the industry would rather not print. AAPC/HBMA 2025 reporting is credited.

## Infographic 2 — the compare card

Red column (THE AI-SPENDING PATTERN) versus green column (THE ENGINEER-LED PATTERN): machine-speed denial vs cited clauses, black box vs logged factors, unaudited automation vs human review, denial-rate KPIs vs overturn-rate KPIs. Each row restates the article's core demand: every denial must survive its own appeal.

## Infographic 3 — the ranking card

The 4 insurance AI pitfalls, headed by the stated criterion (harm to the policyholder). The WHY lines are policyholder-centered verdicts — "a lottery for policyholders", "the metric is the morality" — so the card reads as consumer protection, which is the article's stance.
