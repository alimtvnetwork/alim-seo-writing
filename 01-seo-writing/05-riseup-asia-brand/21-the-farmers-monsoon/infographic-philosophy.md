Visual philosophy for The Farmer's Monsoon.

One rule governs all three frames: look at the whole sky first. Deep navy ground, gold for the leading figure, plain system sans-serif with wide margins and breathing room. One idea per frame, nothing rushed.

infographic-1.svg: "Farmers Using Gen AI" — two bars comparing global adoption (17%) against Latin America (26%), the McKinsey finding that frames the story.

infographic-2.svg: "The AI Adoption Gap" — two bars setting large farms (60%) against small and medium farms (20-25%), because the technology is reaching the farms that need it least.

infographic-3.svg: "Farmer-First Modeling" — a 4-step flow (encode farmer knowledge, retrieval layer, evaluate vs farmer calls, agronomist decides) that puts the knees before the model.
