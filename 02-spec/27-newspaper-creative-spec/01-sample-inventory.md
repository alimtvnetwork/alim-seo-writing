# 01 — Sample Inventory

Element-by-element anatomy of `sample-design-monks-ad.png`
(Design Monks — Lead Marketing Manager hiring ad, Bengali).

## Canvas

- Portrait poster, roughly 4:5. Single centred content column over a
  full-bleed newspaper page.
- The page behind shows 3 faded news columns with vertical column rules;
  the content column sits on the middle column's whitespace.

## Block 1 — Brand headline

- "Design Monks" in extra-bold rounded Latin sans, ink black, ~2x body size.
- Followed by the Bengali particle "এ" (at) in the same weight — the
  code-switch is the hook: English brand, Bengali grammar.
- A yellow highlighter swoosh underlines "Design Monks" only, hand-drawn
  feel, slightly tilted.

## Block 2 — Role announcement

- "লীড মার্কেটিং ম্যানেজার নিচ্ছি!" (Hiring a Lead Marketing Manager!)
  in a heavy Bengali display face, two lines, centred.
- Each line sits on its own rough yellow highlighter stroke — edges are
  uneven, strokes slightly overshoot the text. This is the loudest element
  on the page.
- Flanked left and right by hand-drawn double-slash marks ("//"), like
  emphasis quotes scribbled in the margin.

## Block 3 — Salary hook

- "স্যালারি" (Salary) in a lighter Bengali weight — a quiet label that
  sets up the punch.
- "১ লাখ পর্যন্ত!" (Up to 1 lakh!) in the heavy display face, largest
  numerals on the page. "১ লাখ" backed by a yellow highlighter stroke;
  a thick red hand-drawn underline runs beneath the whole line.

## Block 4 — Referral bonus

- "সাকসেসফুল রেফার করলে" (On a successful referral) in medium weight.
- "পাবেন ১০ হাজার টাকা বোনাস!" (get a 10-thousand-taka bonus!) with
  "১০ হাজার" highlighted yellow.
- Flanked by hand-drawn chevrons ("<" ">") pointing inward at the bonus line.

## Block 5 — CTA

- Black button with rough, grungy edges (not a clean rounded rect).
- White Bengali text "বিস্তারিত কমেন্টে" (Details in the comments) plus
  a solid yellow downward triangle — "look below".

## What makes it work

1. **One idea per block**, stacked in reading order: who → what →
   money → more money → action.
2. **The highlighter is the hierarchy.** Whatever is highlighted yellow is
   what the eye reads: brand, role, salary figure, bonus figure.
3. **Hand-drawn ornaments** (slashes, chevrons, red underline, grungy
   button) signal "a human marked this up" — urgency without clip-art.
4. **The background stays quiet.** The newsprint is faded enough that
   black text on it passes contrast everywhere.
