# Infographic philosophy — The Dentist's Chair

Clinical calm: cool whites and soft blues, generous whitespace, numbers set large like dosage instructions. Nothing glows or shouts; the graphics read like a well-kept patient chart.

- infographic-2.svg: the story's ranked 4 clinic AI missteps, in story order.
- infographic-3.svg: three key statistics on dental no-shows and reminders.
