# Infographic Philosophy — The Baker's Oven

Visual approach: the adoption-versus-impact gap, the heat-map-versus-baker contrast, and the lessons ranked by tuition cost. Navy header, high contrast, one claim per card, footer on each.

- infographic-1 (the 6% stat): McKinsey's high-performer figure set huge against the 88% adoption line — the gap the whole story explains.
- infographic-2 (two paths): last-year's-Tuesdays forecasting on the red side; weather features, logged overrides, and weekly waste measurement on the green side.
- infographic-3 (the ranking): the 4 bakery AI lessons numbered by how much each costs when learned the expensive way.
