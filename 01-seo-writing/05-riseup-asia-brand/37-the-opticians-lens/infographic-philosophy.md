# Infographic Philosophy — The Optician's Lens

Visual approach: spending scale first, then the mirror-versus-measurement contrast, then the vanity-project ranking. Same system as the series: navy header, one idea per card, short labels, footer on every card.

- infographic-1 (the $64B stat): Gartner's 2026 AI platforms/models spend set huge, with the 63.4% growth line underneath — scale the reader can feel.
- infographic-2 (two paths): the glowing tablet's promises on the red side, the engineer's measured outcomes on the green side.
- infographic-3 (the ranking): the 4 retail AI vanity projects numbered by how much they polish the mirror instead of fixing the measurement.
