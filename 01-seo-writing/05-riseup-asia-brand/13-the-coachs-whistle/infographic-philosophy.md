# Infographic Philosophy — The Coach's Whistle

Visual approach: a single devastating number, a side-by-side moral contrast, and the story's ranking distilled to headlines. Navy header, generous whitespace, high contrast, no decoration that competes with the data. Every card ends with the series footer.

- infographic-1 (the 95% stat): the MIT GenAI Divide number set huge, because one honest percentage beats a paragraph of vendor prose.
- infographic-2 (two paths): red X marks for the AI-spending path, green checks for the engineer-led path — the story's compare/contrast as a glanceable verdict.
- infographic-3 (the ranking): the 5 sports AI overclaims as numbered headlines, each with its one-line why, in the narrator's editorial order.
