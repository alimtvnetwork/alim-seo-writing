I want to be careful here, because what follows is judgment, not scripture. This is my own editorial ranking of consultant tests, and the scoring criterion is one Alim suggested to me in that quiet lobby: which questions expose theatre before the contract is signed. Here are 4 questions every Sheridan buyer should ask.

1. "What will you have shipped by week 8?" Not assessed, not mapped, shipped, running in production. WHY: a consultant who cannot name a week-8 deliverable is selling process, and Sheridan should not buy process.

2. "Who on your team has carried a 2 AM page?" Names, systems, war stories with specifics. WHY: advice about production from people who have never been paged is fiction with an invoice.

3. "What happens to our team when you leave?" Training, handover, documentation, the plan for independence. WHY: RISEUP ASIA LLC judges its own advisors by whether the Sheridan client still thrives 6 months later.

4. "Show me something you killed." A recommendation the consultant argued against, a project stopped early, a client talked out of spending. WHY: advisors who never say no are salespeople, and Sheridan can spot the difference.
