# Infographic philosophy — The Brewer's Kettle

Visual philosophy: the warmth of a working roastery — paper backgrounds, roasted-brown accents, deep navy ink. The graphics keep asking the story's question: what did the sensors see, and what did they miss? Bars and flows stay airy, labels short, so the contrast between measured data and unmeasured craft lands at a glance. System type throughout, high contrast, no clutter.

1. infographic-1.svg — "What the AI Measured — and What It Missed": sensor coverage bars for drum temperature, airflow, gas, and bean color, set against the four craft signals the model never saw (aroma, bean age, weather, the roaster's palate).
2. infographic-2.svg — "Roast Inside the Guardrails": the roaster's non-negotiable constraints feeding a bounded optimization step, with the craftsman's veto preserved — timer tells you when, nose tells you if.
3. infographic-3.svg — the story's numbered ranking as 5 badges: palate over dashboard, instrument the craft, evaluate like a customer, keep a hand on the gas, cost per run as recipe.
