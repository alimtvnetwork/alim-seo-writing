Here is my personal ranking of the 4 evaluation gates every Sheridan AI pilot needs. The criterion is simple. Which gate catches the most expensive self-deception?

1. Baseline before build. Measure the current process before the AI touches it. Consequently, a Sheridan pilot without a baseline cannot claim an improvement, only an anecdote.
2. Business metric, not model metric. In contrast, accuracy on a test set is lattice work. A Sheridan CFO cares about cost per resolved case, cycle time, and error rate. Measure those.
3. Cost-per-run tracking from day 1. Inference bills compound silently. The Sheridan pilot that tracks cost per outcome from the 1st deployment never gets a surprise invoice.
4. Human review sampling on a schedule. Inevitably, models drift, data shifts, and edge cases multiply. A Sheridan team that samples outputs weekly catches the soup filling before the customers taste it.
